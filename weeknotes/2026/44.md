## Ingestion

Two main ingestion workstreams going on at the moment:

1) We are currently building a new ingester for LoRaWAN sensors. Data is structured in a different manner to other FRDI sensors - one message can contain data from multiple sensors - so it requires some thought about how its ingested into our current setup.

2) We are transferring to a new partition structure in our `ingested` and `processed` buckets. This will provide consistency between networks and aligns better with the incoming data (see the [discussion](https://github.com/NERC-CEH/fdri_discussions/discussions/2))

This involves changes across most of our repos and requires careful thought to make sure users still have access to all the data during the transition. Our `staging` account is the perfect testbed for how this will be rolled out in production.
